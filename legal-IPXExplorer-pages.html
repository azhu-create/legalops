Implementation Cheatsheet

How to Build This in Google Sites

1. The "Image-as-Button" Trick (Top 4 Shortcuts)

Google Sites does not have native bordered cards with drop shadows. To get the sleek app tiles in Templates 1 & 2:

  • Create 4 small icon graphics (or full rectangular tiles) in Google Slides or Canva and export as transparent PNGs.
  • Drop a 4-Column Content Block onto your page, upload each PNG, click the image, and click the Link (🔗) icon to turn the entire graphic into a button.

2. Use the 3 Section Styles for Visual Rhythm

In Google Sites, click the paint palette icon on the left edge of any horizontal band to alternate between Style 1 (White), Style 2 (Soft Tint), and Style 3 (Bold Brand Fill). Alternating these stops the page from looking like a long white Google Doc.

3. Handling 5 Practice Areas on a 12-Column Grid

Because 5 items don't divide evenly into 12 columns, each template solves this natively:

  • Template 1 (3 + 2 Centered): Put 3 items in Row 1 (4 cols each). In Row 2, drag the 4th and 5th blocks to the center so 2 empty columns sit on the left and right.
  • Template 2 (4 + 8 Split): Put the intro blurb in a 4-column block on the left, and stack the 5 areas on the right.
  • Template 3 (5 Horizontal Rows): Use 5 stacked rows (2-col icon on left + 10-col text on right) separated by native Dividers. Zero alignment headaches!

4. Native Collapsible Groups (Self-Service Library)

In the right-hand Insert panel, click Collapsible group. Create three side-by-side 4-column collapsible groups (Forms & Contract Review, Playbooks & Guides, Company Policies) so employees can expand only what they need.

5. Urgent Matters Banner (Embed Code Block)

To get the red/amber left-bordered warning box for Urgent Matters, click Insert → Embed → Embed code and paste a lightweight HTML snippet with inline styles so it stands out from routine links.
