Phase 2: Ingestion & System Allocations

A. The "Matter-Level Fix" Rule for Stampli Syncs

CRITICAL: If an invoice is submitted with the wrong GL code or allocation, you MUST fix the allocation at the Matter Level in Brightflag. If you only fix it on the individual invoice, the downstream API sync to Stampli will break on every subsequent invoice submitted to that matter.

B. Understanding Allocation Parameters

  • Entity & Project ID: If a cost benefits two project phases (e.g., Easley I and II), split it 50/50. Exception: Land/Option Task Codes (1.10, 1.15, 1.90) are strictly kept 100% to one entity.
  • GL Code: Projects pre-COD are capitalized to 1710 - Project Assets. Transactional Deals use 2601 - Upfront Financing Costs.

B. The Brightflag Review Checklist (For Matter Leads & Attorneys)

When routing invoices for Stage 2/3 approvals, ensure reviewers are checking against the official Legal checklist:

  • Work Verification: Verifying work completed is what was actually ordered.
  • In-House Capability: Taking note of line items that could be performed in-house for future engagements.
  • Excessive Time: Reviewing for excessive time allocations based on the description.
  • Staffing Levels: Reviewing the matter is staffed with the appropriate number and level of personnel.
  • No "Learning Time": Confirming no time entries for "learning time" required to bring a new attorney or legal assistant up to speed.
  • Correct Matter: Confirming the invoice was submitted to the correct Brightflag matter.

Phase 4: Stampli Workflow & AP Processing

Once an invoice is fully approved in Brightflag, it is pushed via API to Stampli for final Accounts Payable processing. Legal Ops must understand how Stampli handles these imports to troubleshoot payment delays.

A. Stampli Approver Capabilities

  • Direct Coding Edits (New Policy): Instead of rejecting an invoice due to bad coding, Stampli approvers can now directly edit coding fields (GL Account, Location, Task Code, Project, Department) on the fly.
  • Ask a Question: If an approver is unsure about a charge, they should use the "Ask a Question" feature in Stampli rather than rejecting the invoice, which restarts the entire lifecycle.

B. Why a Stampli Invoice Would Be Rejected (Not Approved)

Invoices exported from Brightflag to Stampli are often delayed or rejected by AP/Approvers for the following reasons:

  • Incorrect Amount (Billed Total vs. Approved Fees): Brightflag users sometimes push the "Approved Fees" or "Billed Fees" to Stampli. Stampli requires the absolute Billed Total amount.
  • OCR Mismatches (Wrong Vendor or Invoice #): Stampli's Optical Character Recognition (OCR) sometimes misreads the vendor name or invoice number on the PDF. If the data pushed from Brightflag doesn't match the OCR read, AP will kick it back.
  • The Wrong Rejection Reason (Critical Trap): If an invoice *must* be rejected for coding, the approver must select "Incorrect Coding (send back to the coder)". Selecting any other rejection reason permanently sends the invoice to the Stampli Trash/Rejected queue where AP coders have no access to it, forcing the firm to resubmit entirely.

Phase 5: Financial Policies, Tiers & Exceptions

  • Delegation of Authority Tiers: Tier 5 ($25k limit), Tier 4 ($250k limit), Tier 3 ($1M limit), Tier 2 ($5M limit - Dept Heads), Tier 1 (Unlimited - C-Suite).
  • The $100k Prepaids Exception: Invoices under $100,000 do NOT need to be amortized to prepaid accounts (1400/1405). Expense them directly to the current operational month.
  • Under $5k Auto-Approvals: Department heads can auto-approve invoices well below their tier limits without triggering escalations.
