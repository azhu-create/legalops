2. Granular Allocations & GL Coding Rules

Accurate coding at the matter level dictates how NetSuite processes the invoice.

A. Easley Project Splits & Allocations

  • The 50/50 Standard: Commercial, hedging, and general project costs are split exactly 50/50 between IP Easley, LLC (Easley I) and IP Easley II, LLC (Easley II).
  • No-Split Exceptions: Accounting dictates that Land/Option Task Codes (1.10, 1.15, 1.90, 1.99) are strictly kept "Full" (100% to one entity) and NEVER split.

C. Capitalization vs. Operational Expenses

  • Pre-COD (1710 - Project Assets): Projects under development/construction (like Easley) must capitalize setup fees to the balance sheet. Do not use 5xxx operating expenses for pre-COD hedges or setups.
  • Post-COD: Once Substantial Completion is reached, expenses transition to 5xxx series operating accounts.

4. Financial Policies, Tiers & Exceptions

Brightflag enforces the CFO's Payment Approvals Policy thresholds. Exceeding a tier limit forces an escalation to the next level up.

A. Payment Approval Tiers (Delegation of Authority)

  • Tier 5: $25k limit (Standard FTEs)
  • Tier 4: $250k limit (Senior Leads / Ops Personnel)
  • Tier 3: $1M limit (Senior Dept Managers)
  • Tier 2: $5M limit (Department Heads, e.g., Colin Cardwell, Paul Vercruyssen)
  • Tier 1: Unlimited (C-Suite, e.g., Todd Johansen - CCO)

B. Critical Policy Exceptions

  • The $100k Prepaids Exception: Invoices under $100,000 do NOT need to be amortized to prepaid accounts (1400/1405). Expense them directly to the current operational month.
  • Under $5k Auto-Approvals: Department heads can auto-approve invoices well below their tier limits without triggering escalations.
  • Under $5k IT/Furniture Rule: Small office/remote equipment is never capitalized. Code as: Category: Exp. Furn. & Fixtures Under $5k | GL: 6xxx series | Project: Non-Project (A91) | Task: GA/Allocated Costs.
  • Professional Development: Capped at $2,000 annually for individuals, $5,000 for Executives.

5. Law Firm & Timekeeper Compliance (AI Controls)

  • Rate Locking: You must lock in approved timekeeper rates during onboarding.
  • Automated AI Rejections: Brightflag's AI automatically pre-screens and instantly rejects invoices containing unapproved timekeepers or rates exceeding locked thresholds before they hit Stage 1.
